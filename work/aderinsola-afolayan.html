Overview

A personal life-archive site organised around discrete collections — story, interests, journal, places, things being learned, a future log — with a 'random memory' entry point instead of a conventional homepage.

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript Random-Entry Generator

The Challenge

A personal site with no product, service or commercial goal still needs a reason for a stranger to keep browsing past the homepage.

Outcome

Organising the archive into discrete, differently-shaped collections — a journal, a places log, a things-learning list — gave a personal site the browsing structure normally reserved for larger platforms.
